STAGE 10: ENTRY VALIDATION

OBJECTIVE

Validate whether a trade idea is usable based on direction, risk, POI and confluence.

RULES

- A valid bullish entry must have bullish direction, a bullish POI, and a valid risk structure.
- A valid bearish entry must have bearish direction, a bearish POI, and a valid risk structure.
- Stop-loss and take-profit checks must be consistent with the order of price action.
- No future data or lookahead is allowed.

IMPORTANT

- The validator should report clear pass/fail checks.
- Entry validation must remain a decision-support utility, not auto-execution logic.

This stage completes the next phase of the historical-only engine.
