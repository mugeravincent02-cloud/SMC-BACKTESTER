STAGE 05: LIQUIDITY SWEEP DETECTION

OBJECTIVE

Detect when price takes liquidity and rejects/reclaims the level.

BUY-SIDE LOGIC

Potential bullish sweep:

1. A known SSL exists.
2. Price trades below the SSL.
3. Price subsequently reclaims/closes back above the relevant level according to the documented strategy.

SELL-SIDE LOGIC

Potential bearish sweep:

1. A known BSL exists.
2. Price trades above the BSL.
3. Price subsequently closes/reclaims below the relevant level according to the documented strategy.

IMPORTANT

A liquidity sweep is NOT automatically an entry.

The detector should report the event and supporting data.

Include:

- liquidity type
- level
- sweep candle index
- sweep candle time
- relevant price
- direction
- confirmation/reclaim information

Do not use future candles.

Test bullish and bearish examples.

Test false/failed sweeps.

Update documentation.

Do not proceed to Stage 06.
