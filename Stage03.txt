STAGE 03: MARKET STRUCTURE ENGINE

OBJECTIVE

Build a deterministic and testable market-structure layer.

AUDIT AND IMPLEMENT

1. SwingDetector

2. StructureDetector

3. BOSDetector

4. CHOCHDetector

RULES

Swings must be deterministic.

Structure classification must be based only on available historical candles.

BOS must not use future candles.

CHoCH must not be treated as identical to BOS.

Do not invent additional structure rules.

TEST CASES

Create deterministic candle fixtures for:

1. Bullish structure.
2. Bearish structure.
3. Structure reversal.
4. Clear BOS.
5. Clear bearish BOS.
6. Clear CHoCH.
7. Insufficient candles.
8. Empty candles.
9. Equal/ambiguous structures.

Verify indexes and candle references.

Verify chronological ordering.
