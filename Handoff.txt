SMC STRATEGY BASIS

The strategy follows the principle:

"Identify Liquidity or Become Liquidity."

BUY CONCEPT

A potential buy setup begins after bearish liquidity is taken.

Examples of SSL:

- Swing low
- Low of Day
- Previous Day Low
- Previous Week Low
- Session low
- Equal lows
- Retail support
- Trendline liquidity

After liquidity is taken, price should react around a bullish Order Block / relevant POI.

Lower timeframe confirmation can include:

- Bullish BOS
- Bullish CHoCH
- Supply-to-demand flip
- Single-candle mitigation

SELL CONCEPT

A potential sell setup is the inverse.

Examples of BSL:

- Swing high
- High of Day
- Previous Day High
- Previous Week High
- Session high
- Equal highs
- Retail resistance
- Trendline liquidity

After liquidity is taken, price should react around a bearish Order Block / relevant POI.

Lower timeframe confirmation can include:

- Bearish BOS
- Bearish CHoCH
- Demand-to-supply flip
- Single-candle mitigation

TIMEFRAME CONCEPT

HTF is used for:

- Trend
- Major structure
- POIs
- Liquidity context

LTF is used for:

- Confirmation
- Entry validation

Example:

HTF: 4H / 1H
LTF: 15M / 5M / 1M

Do not hard-code one timeframe relationship unless the existing strategy documentation explicitly requires it.

ORDER BLOCK WORKING DEFINITION

The current working definition is:

"Order block means where we enter the market for buys and sells. The order block happens when price creates an imbalance and takes out the previous candle high. This happens after a shift in market direction from either a bearish trend to a bullish trend and vice versa."

Do not silently replace this definition with a generic internet definition.

BOS WORKING CONCEPT

A BOS occurs when price breaks a relevant previous structural high or low and closes beyond it.

Example:

Price creates a structural high, retraces, then returns and breaks the previous high and closes above it.

CHoCH

CHoCH represents a meaningful change in market structure and should be treated separately from ordinary continuation BOS.

LIQUIDITY

Important liquidity concepts include:

- Equal Highs
- Equal Lows
- Previous Day High
- Previous Day Low
- Previous Week High
- Previous Week Low
- Session High
- Session Low
- Swing Highs
- Swing Lows

RISK

The strategy documentation describes:

Personal account risk:
1% to 2% per trade.

Funded account risk:
0.25% to 1%.

Stops should be based on the setup and relevant structure/liquidity.

The strategy also discusses moving the stop to breakeven after favorable structural progress.

Do not hard-code these values into the engine unless configuration requires them.
