STAGE 09: HTF / LTF ANALYSIS

OBJECTIVE

Connect higher-timeframe context with lower-timeframe confirmation.

HTF PROVIDES

- trend
- major structure
- liquidity
- POIs

LTF PROVIDES

- reaction
- BOS
- CHoCH
- entry confirmation

IMPLEMENT

1. Configurable HTF.
2. Configurable LTF.
3. HTF structure analysis.
4. HTF POI detection.
5. LTF reaction.
6. LTF BOS/CHoCH confirmation.
7. Time alignment.

IMPORTANT

Do not mix candles from different timeframes incorrectly.

Do not use future HTF information.

Do not allow an LTF candle to confirm something before the HTF information existed.

Test chronological alignment carefully.

Update docs.

Do not proceed to Stage 10.
