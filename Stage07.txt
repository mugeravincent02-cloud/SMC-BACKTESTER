STAGE 07: FAIR VALUE GAP / IMBALANCE DETECTION

OBJECTIVE

Detect historical-only price gaps and imbalances that represent a fair value gap.

BUY-SIDE LOGIC

A bullish FVG exists when a candle breaks above the previous candle's high without a continuous fill between the two candles.

SELL-SIDE LOGIC

A bearish FVG exists when a candle breaks below the previous candle's low without a continuous fill between the two candles.

IMPORTANT

- Do not use future candles.
- Keep the detector deterministic and historical-only.
- Return the relevant gap boundaries and direction.
- Test both bullish and bearish gaps.
- Update the baseline and docs.

Do not proceed to Stage 08 until the Stage 07 test contract passes.
