STAGE 08: POINTS OF INTEREST (POI)

OBJECTIVE

Group the most relevant structure and imbalance zones into a points-of-interest list.

RULES

- Use valid FVGs, liquidity levels and nearby structure references.
- Keep the result directional and deterministic.
- Do not invent entries from invalid or conflicting zones.
- Prefer historical-only references and existing data already available.

IMPORTANT

- A POI is not an entry.
- A POI must be supported by a valid directional zone.
- Stage 08 depends on the Stage 07 FVG contract.

Do not proceed to Stage 09 until the Stage 08 test contract passes.
STAGE 08: POINT OF INTEREST ENGINE

OBJECTIVE

Combine SMC components into meaningful Points of Interest.

Potential components:

- Order Block
- FVG
- Liquidity
- Liquidity sweep
- Market structure
- Previous day liquidity
- Previous week liquidity
- Session liquidity

IMPORTANT

Do not create an arbitrary numerical score unless the strategy explicitly defines one.

Instead, expose the actual reasons a POI exists.

A POI should contain:

- direction
- zone
- supporting components
- liquidity relationship
- structure relationship
- timestamp/index references
- validity state
- reasons

Do not turn every detected SMC element into a trade.

Test confluence and non-confluence cases.

Update documentation.

Wait for approval.
