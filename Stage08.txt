STAGE 08: POINT OF INTEREST ENGINE

OBJECTIVE

Combine SMC components into meaningful Points of Interest.

Potential components:

- Order Block
- FVG
- Liquidity
- Liquidity sweep
- Market structure
- Previous day liquidity
- Previous week liquidity
- Session liquidity

IMPORTANT

Do not create an arbitrary numerical score unless the strategy explicitly defines one.

Instead, expose the actual reasons a POI exists.

A POI should contain:

- direction
- zone
- supporting components
- liquidity relationship
- structure relationship
- timestamp/index references
- validity state
- reasons

Do not turn every detected SMC element into a trade.

Test confluence and non-confluence cases.

Update documentation.

Wait for approval.
