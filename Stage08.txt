STAGE 08: POINTS OF INTEREST (POI)

OBJECTIVE

Group the most relevant structure and imbalance zones into a points-of-interest list.

RULES

- Use valid FVGs, liquidity levels and nearby structure references.
- Keep the result directional and deterministic.
- Do not invent entries from invalid or conflicting zones.
- Prefer historical-only references and existing data already available.

IMPORTANT

- A POI is not an entry.
- A POI must be supported by a valid directional zone.
- Stage 08 depends on the Stage 07 FVG contract.

Do not proceed to Stage 09 until the Stage 08 test contract passes.
