STAGE 12: BACKTESTING ENGINE

OBJECTIVE

Create the chronological backtesting engine.

ARCHITECTURE

Separate:

1. Market data.
2. Strategy analysis.
3. Trade simulation.
4. Statistics.

BACKTEST RULES

Process candles chronologically.

At candle N, the engine may only know information available by candle N.

No lookahead bias.

No future swing confirmation leaking backward.

No future liquidity information.

No future Order Block information.

No future FVG information.

Implement:

- entry
- stop loss
- take profit
- exit
- trade lifecycle
- chronological processing

Handle same-candle SL/TP ambiguity explicitly.

If both SL and TP are touched within one candle and intrabar order cannot be known, do not silently assume the favorable result.

Create deterministic fixtures.

Test:

- winning trade
- losing trade
- no trade
- multiple trades
- same-candle SL/TP
- end-of-data open position

Update documentation.

Do not proceed to Stage 13.
